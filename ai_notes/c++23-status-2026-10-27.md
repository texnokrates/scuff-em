  libStaticSolver PostProcessing, scuff-static EPFile loop.  Fill
  mode: scuff-transmission GetFlux function-static field matrices are
  now static `unique_ptr`s.
- Conversions fixed three latent leaks: the returned `FMatrix` was
  never freed in scuff-rf `ProcessEPFile`, in the scuff-static EPFile
  loop (along with its `X` matrix), and in scuff-scatter's EPFile
  path.
- Also fixed: a latent null-deref in the wrong-size-XMatrix `ErrExit`

**Ownership-discipline conversion, remaining dual-mode assembly entry
points (2026-10-30, `48cdb900` + `54e0615d`):**

the same scheme (allocating overload -> `unique_ptr`, filling overload
-> reference, wrong-size caller buffer -> `ErrExit`):

- `StaticSolver::AssembleRHSVector` (both overloads): fill-only

  PlateWithHole.

  all 11 callers allocated via AllocateBEMMatrix and ignored the
  return.  The old wrong-size path warned and replaced the caller's
  matrix WITHOUT deleting it (leak) and returned the replacement
  (dangling for fill callers).  The `SCUFF_MATRIX_2018` env-gated path
